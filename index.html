Troubleshooting

Microphone access is not working

The app requires microphone permission to analyze live audio. If access was denied, open iOS Settings, find Your Smart Listening (Pro), and enable Microphone access.

Sound change alerts are not arriving

Confirm notifications are enabled both in the app and in iOS Settings. If alerts are still missing, make sure the app has permission to send notifications and that Focus or notification summary settings are not delaying delivery.

I want to track different sounds

Open Detection Settings to choose from the core sound labels, or use Advanced Settings to browse and enable raw audio classes for more detailed sound monitoring.

The app is not detecting sounds the way I expect

Detection quality can vary with background noise, distance from the source, device position, and overlapping sounds. Try moving closer to the sound source and reducing competing noise in the environment.

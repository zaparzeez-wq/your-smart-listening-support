Your Smart Listening (Pro)

This policy explains how the app uses audio access and what information is handled while you use the app.

Summary

Your Smart Listening (Pro) uses microphone access to analyze sound in real time so the app can identify sound categories and audio events selected by the user.

Audio Access

The app requests access to the microphone so it can process nearby audio and classify sounds such as speech, sirens, baby crying, music, and other supported audio classes.

Microphone access is optional, but core detection features will not work without it.

How Audio Is Processed

Audio is processed on device to generate sound classification results used by the app interface and notifications.

This support site does not describe any server-side audio processing because the current app experience is designed around on-device detection.

Information Stored by the App

The app may store your preferences, such as enabled sound categories and notification settings, on your device so your choices persist between sessions.

The app may also keep recent detection history on device to support in-app history views.

Information Sharing

The app is not intended to transmit raw microphone audio to the developer as part of normal use.

If you contact support, any information you choose to send by email, including screenshots or diagnostic details, will be handled as part of that support request.

Your Choices

You can stop audio access at any time by disabling microphone permission in iOS Settings. You can also turn notifications on or off in the app and in iOS Settings.
